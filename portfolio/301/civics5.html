Utilitarianism Ethics

Intentions don't matter. Good outcome = good actions. Actions should be measured in terms of the happiness or pleasure they produce. Happiness is our final end, its why we do anything at all. This is what should drive our morality. But we should do this to everyone's end, Utilitarianism considers all people's happiness in it's balancing. Most pleasure for most people. Sometimes you have to sacrifice your own pleasure for many other's pleasure. Everyone is biased though, so instead of making moral decisions from 'our own' perspective we should take the perspective of a benevolent, uninterested spector. (ok yeah no duh easy for you to say)

No moral theory should demand taking an innocent life.

Rule Utilitarianism

A more stringent version, pretty much. kill 100 people to save 101. Sometimes this means you kill innocents. In the short term, it might hurt lots of people. But if in the long term, happiness is still net positive, you should do it.

  • In any given situation, you should choose the action that produces the greatest good for the greatest number.
