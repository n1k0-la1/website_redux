Thomas Hobbs' theory of government is one of Contractarianism. he posits that the natural state of nature is "solitary, poor, nasty, brutish and short". However, Free Self-intrested rational individuals will naturally enter a shared agreement to exchange some freedoms and preform some duties in exchange for the benefits of living a society. Morals are not innate, but instead stem from this social contract. One opposition to this philosophy is "Defection", the betrayal of the contract for one's self interests. This theory assumes we get to choose what responsibilities we incur.

Contracts

The solution for this is a contract (shared agreement). Inevitably (?) Free Self-Interested Rational will enter this contract with eachother. You trade in natural freedoms, and agree to duties such as taxes, jury duty, getting punished for violations. In exchange for securities offered by society/a cooperative living.

there are implicit and explicit contracts. A citizen born in the us has agreed to an implicit contract, but an immigrant has explicitly agreed to a contract. It is still reasonable and fair for you to obey an implicit contract because you still reap the benefits. However, in order for a contract to be valid, the contractors must be Free, ie. slavery is not valid under contractarianism.

Even though there may be some rules that are unfair to you, over all as a whole the system benefits your way of life.

Assumption: we get to choose what responsibilities we incur, so we should stringently follow our agreements.

Morality

'True Morality' not real but stems from people's shared agreement. Right acts do not violate the free rational agreements we've made; and Wrong immoral acts violate the agreement.

Morality becomes real when we agree to it, just as rules do. [^1]Over the course of time, people's opinions may change, which will change the law and the morality of that time.
