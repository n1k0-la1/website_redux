The state		 1. Washington State Legislature
2. The Washington State Legislature is the state's lawmaking body, where elected Senators and Representatives come together to create and pass laws that shape the daily lives of Washingtonians. The lower house goes into committee (leg.wa.gov)
3. It is made of two separate assemblies, the Lower House of Representatives (98 reps) and the Upper State Senate (49 senators + lieutenant governor as president)
4.		 1.The Washington State Judicial Branch
2.Each level of the Washington State Judicial Branch has different specific duties. The Courts of limited Jurisdiction oversee smaller cases on a local level. This is where many cases such as misdemeanors, traffic infractions, and small claims are held. The Superior Courts oversee felonies, family law cases, juvenile proceedings, or cases where the amount in consideration is more than $75K. If a case is appealed, it then goes to the Court of Appeals. The Court of Appeals must hear all appeals that are filed to it. On the highest level is the Supreme Court, who review the decisions of all the other courts. They can choose which cases to take, usually cases that concern major constitutional questions, or issues that affect many of its citizens.
3.It is made up of 4 levels: The WA state Supreme Court; the Court of Appeals; the Superior Courts; and the Courts of Limited Jurisdiction (which are the District Courts and the Municipal Courts).
4.The judicial branch can declare laws made by the legislative branch unconstitutional. Federal court decisions override state court decisions when they are conflicting
